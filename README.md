# 💊 Global Pharmacy Sales Analysis (2020 - 2025)

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI analytics project provides an in-depth evaluation of global pharmacy daily transactions spanning from 2020 to 2025. Analyzing over **177,000+ transaction records** across **19 countries**, the dashboard delivers key business insights into sales revenue, category growth, inventory levels, and pandemic-era demand shifts.

---

## 📊 Key Highlights & Dashboard Features
* **Sales & Revenue Performance:** Tracks total revenue, units sold, and average price variations across multi-year cycles.
* **Geographical & Regional Trends:** Interactive breakdowns across global markets including South Asia, Middle East, Europe, North & South America.
* **Category & Medicine Analysis:** Performance tracking for primary product classes such as Chronic care, Vitamins, Antibiotics, Cough & Cold, and Antipyretics.
* **Inventory & Expiry Risk Management:** Monitors stock levels and remaining days to expiry to minimize wastage and supply bottlenecks.
* **COVID-19 Impact Insights:** Evaluates demand fluctuations during peak pandemic vs. post-pandemic periods.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Business Intelligence & Visualization:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformation & Modeling:** Power Query, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* **Dataset:** Daily Pharmacy Sales CSV (`global_pharmacy_sales_2020_2025_daily_dataset.csv`)
